Three equally sized landscape cinematic frames stacked vertically, crisp narrow black gutters, overall portrait composition. NO text, captions, letters, UI, logos, typography, numbers, watermark. Direction A: tangible traces of thinking, paper becoming light. Each frame is mostly absolute black, centered subject, extreme negative space, premium startup launch-film visual craft, restrained photographic materials, warm ivory and silver with only the final frame gaining subtle pale seafoam and amber. TOP FRAME: in an infinite black void, a small central suspended layered cluster of thin translucent presentation pages, physically convincing paper edges and subtle graphite erasure/revision traces, no readable writing, no stock-photo desk or hands. Some pages recede backwards, some are incomplete ahead: past experience, present self-questioning, future possibility implied without literal time symbols. Intimate chiaroscuro grazing light, not a document icon or computer interface. MIDDLE FRAME: same paper cluster tightly compressed into a tiny brilliant pearl of light, a few recognizable paper-edge filaments transforming into fine luminous dust and individual star points radiating outward. Dramatic moment of ignition without blowing out the frame; intensely black surrounding space, elegant volumetric light, no conventional lens-flare cross or planet. BOTTOM FRAME: the dust and hundreds of individual star points now gathered into three subtly connected irregular nebular clouds, rich fine-grained smoky texture, luminous cores, organic uneven shapes, distinct clusters with black channels between them. Sparse pale seafoam, muted warm amber, silver-white. These clouds must read as a gathering of individual sparks rather than generic astronomy wallpaper. Centered dramatic editorial composition, restrained and emotionally credible, not fantasy poster, no astronauts, no people silhouettes, no neural-brain imagery. Cohesive materials and continuity across all three frames. This is a visual brainstorm, not actual source data.

## 최종 프롬프트 B

Use case: stylized-concept. Generate ONE premium experimental startup keynote cinematic storyboard, three equal landscape frames stacked vertically in a portrait triptych, narrow pure black gutters. Absolutely no text, letters, titles, UI, labels, numerals, logos, watermark. Direction B: only light remains. Radically abstract, immaculate black void, centered suspended luminous forms with generous negative space. Silver-white monochrome until the last frame, when extremely restrained pale jade and amber emerge. This tells the story of reflecting on one's past, core strengths and future, then records becoming people as stars, then individual future tasks gathering into luminous constellations. TOP FRAME: a small, exquisitely fine sculptural knot of silver luminous filaments at dead center, dozens of delicate strands retracing, crossing and undoing their paths, a few trajectories extend tentatively forward, long exposure light drawing with tactile optical fibers and subtle brightness variation. Looks like the visible trace of difficult thought, no brain outline, no human silhouette, no diagram, no paper. Large black empty margins; emotional and contemplative not chaotic. MIDDLE FRAME: the knot collapses into one small intensely bright pearl at center and emits a sudden clean radial constellation of hundreds of tiny discrete ivory sparks, frozen at the magical instant of becoming stars. Silver white, faint pale jade and muted amber. The clusters occupy the central horizontal band with extensive black above and below.
